About me

During my tenure at Cal, I have been a Computational Social Science Training Fellow and a Center on Civility & Democratic Engagement fellow.

Immediately before starting my doctoral training, I earned a Master of Public Policy from the Goldman School of Public Policy at the University of California, Berkeley. I earned my BA from the University of California, Berkeley in political science. I also hold graduate certificates in applied data science and race, public health and social justice. In addition, I have worked as a research assistant at the Berkeley Institute for Young Americans, the Democracy Policy Institute, the Possibility Lab and the Justice Lab with Dr. Nikki Jones.

Professionally, I worked for several years as a political and policy research consultant through all regions of California. During this time, I supported grassroots organizations, youth development, criminal justice reform, Medi-Cal expansion, language access and cultural competency policies, organized labor, anti-displacement advocacy and multi-sector collaboration.

Although I am a triple Bear and have made my home in the East Bay, I am forever a proud native of South Sacramento. As a first-generation college student and former community college transfer student, I am always willing to lend my support to students from similarly marginalized backgrounds. When I am not reading academic research, I can be found enjoying my first love of music, exploring art exhibits, hunting for ube-infused snacks, foraging my way to a spiritual connection with the natural environment and finding balance in all life’s fruitful endeavors as a multidimensional humanoid.
