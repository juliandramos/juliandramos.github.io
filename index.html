Hi, my name is julian! 👋🏽

I am a PhD student in the Health Policy program, Population Health Sciences track, at the University of California, Berkeley. As a computational social scientist, I broadly examine how local political institutions, public policy and social behavior shape criminal legal and public health outcomes within race-class subjugated communities. My doctoral research sits at the intersection of two topics central to public safety and public health.

  1. The impact of institutional changes on exposure to homicidal victimization and how this victimization in turn influences collective efficacy.
  2. Underreporting of the killing of civilians by law enforcement with attention to prediction of such behavior and policies that shape this lack of transparency.

In addition, I have ongoing interests in the health determinants of politics, the local political economy of quality public services, guns, migration and using machine learning methods to complement approaches to causal inference.
